# 🐞 Navbar Social Hover Causes UI Overlap

## Summary
Hovering over social icons in the top navbar shows an additional 3-button bar that overlaps and obstructs the side menu.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Hover over social icons in the blue navbar.
2. Observe the bar that appears under it.

## Expected Result
Minimal UI or tooltip, not large obstructive bar.

## Actual Result
Three social buttons appear again and overlap the sidebar.

## Severity
🟡 Medium – Aesthetic/UI overlap

## Suggestion
Remove duplication or make bar non-overlapping.

## Environment
- Arc V1.50.1
- macOS 15.4.1
